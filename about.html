About

Afenifere Hammed Adewumi is a fintech founder and executive focused on building compliant, scalable payment infrastructure for African businesses operating globally.

As CEO & Co-Founder of Spark Tech Hub, he oversees two fintech products - Oneremit and Payremit - both built to make it easier for businesses in emerging markets to send, receive, and manage cross-border payments. His work spans payment infrastructure, regulatory compliance, and trade facilitation across Canada, the United States, Nigeria, Ghana, and the United Kingdom.

Vision

What has been missing is infrastructure to move money without friction. When payments get easier, trade follows. When emerging markets trade freely with the rest of the world, everyone wins.
