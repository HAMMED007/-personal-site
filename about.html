About

Hammed Adewumi Afenifere (professionally known as Hammed Afenifere, and also registered as Afenifere Hammed Adewumi on certain corporate and licensing documents) is a fintech founder and payments executive focused on building compliant, scalable financial technology infrastructure that unlocks international trade for emerging markets.

As CEO & Co-Founder of Spark Tech Hub, he oversees two payment platforms: Oneremit (focusing on secure, low-friction cross-border payments for emerging market businesses) and Payremit (tailored for the diaspora remittance corridors). His work spans international payment rails, regulatory compliance operations, and cross-border commerce solutions across Canada, the United States, Nigeria, Ghana, and the United Kingdom.

Professional Timeline & Milestones

I have built my career at the intersection of technology, finance, and international regulation:

Vision

What has been missing is infrastructure to move money without friction. When payments get easier, trade follows. When emerging markets trade freely with the rest of the world, everyone wins.
